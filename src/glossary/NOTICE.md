# EDB Economics glossary — notice

`data/edb-economics-2020.json` is a transcription of:

> *An English-Chinese Glossary of Terms Commonly Used in the Teaching of Economics in
> Secondary Schools* (2020). Curriculum Development Institute, Education Bureau, Hong Kong
> Special Administrative Region.
> <https://www.edb.gov.hk/attachment/tc/curriculum-development/kla/pshe/references-and-resources/economics/glossary_for_economics_2020.pdf>

© The Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region. All rights in the glossary
belong to it.

**The glossary is not covered by this app's MIT licence.** It is included for reference, so
the app can check and suggest the Chinese terms Hong Kong schools use. The MIT grant in
`LICENSE` covers the app's own code only.

The file is kept byte-for-byte as transcribed (its sha256 is pinned by
`src/glossary/parse.test.ts`). Corrections and app policy — parsing exceptions, the 住戶
display form, the import family's 進口 preference — live in `src/glossary/overrides.ts`,
never in the data.

Comments on the glossary itself go to the Education Bureau: `info_pshe@edb.gov.hk`.
