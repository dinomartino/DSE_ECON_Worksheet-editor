1.	Which of the following is a characteristic of a public good?

A.	Its consumption is rivalrous.
B.	People can be excluded from using it at a low cost.
C.	It is non-excludable.
D.	It must be provided by the government.

2.	If the price of a good rises by 10% and its quantity demanded falls by 5%, its demand is

A.	perfectly inelastic.
B.	perfectly elastic.
C.	elastic.
D.	inelastic.

3.	Which of the following is a stock concept?

A.	the money supply of Hong Kong
B.	Hong Kong's GDP in 2025
C.	the monthly wage of a clerk
D.	the daily output of a factory

4.	A firm earns zero economic profit. This means that

A.	its total revenue is zero.
B.	its accounting profit is zero.
C.	it covers all its opportunity costs.
D.	it will leave the industry.

5.	Which of the following will be included in Hong Kong's GDP?

A.	a second-hand flat sold in 2025
B.	a meal cooked by a domestic helper and paid for by her employer
C.	government transfer payments to the elderly
D.	shares bought on the stock market

Answers
1. C	2. D	3. A
4. C	5. B
