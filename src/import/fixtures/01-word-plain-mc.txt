Part A: Multiple-choice questions (8 marks)

1.	A café owner in Mong Kok decides to open on Sundays. Which of the following are costs of this decision to her?

(1)	the wages she pays to the Sunday staff
(2)	the rent she already paid for the whole month
(3)	the leisure she gives up on Sundays

A.	(1) and (2) only
B.	(1) and (3) only
C.	(2) and (3) only
D.	(1), (2) and (3)


2.	Which of the following is an example of a free good?

A.	sunlight on a beach in Sai Kung
B.	a free newspaper handed out at an MTR station
C.	tap water supplied by the Water Supplies Department
D.	a free sample of shampoo


3.	The statutory minimum wage (法定最低工資) is raised. Which of the following groups will most likely gain?

(1)	low-skilled workers who keep their jobs
(2)	firms that hire many low-skilled workers
(3)	consumers of labour-intensive services


A.	(1) only	B.	(1) and (2) only	C.	(2) and (3) only	D.	(1), (2) and (3)
