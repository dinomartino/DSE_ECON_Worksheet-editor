Section A (3 marks)

	1.	Tom spends his Saturday evening at a concert instead of working a part-time shift that pays $400.

		The concert ticket costs $600. What is his opportunity cost of attending the concert?

A.	$400
B.	$600
C.	$1 000
D.	the enjoyment of the concert

	2.	Which of the following will shift the demand curve for electric cars to the right?

A.	a fall in the price of electric cars
B.	a rise in the price of petrol
C.	an improvement in battery technology
D.	a rise in the cost of car parts

	3.	Which of the following is a positive statement?

A.	The government should cut salaries tax.
B.	Hong Kong's unemployment rate rose last quarter.
C.	Rents in Hong Kong are too high.
D.	Public housing should be given to the young first.
